Why it's different

Curation, not just a quality score.

Most cullers rank photos by sharpness. Aaria Moments understands the day.

🕯️

Ritual protection

Near-identical frames of different people performing the same ceremony are kept — because each relative's moment matters, not just the first one.

🗓️

Event coverage

Automatically detects each function — betrothal, lunch, reception — and guarantees a minimum from every one, so nothing gets under-represented.
