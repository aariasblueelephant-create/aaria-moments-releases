What it does

Three tools. One finished album.

A focused workflow that mirrors how you already work — clean, identify, curate.

✨

Clean

Removes duplicate frames and out-of-focus shots with adjustable sensitivity. Copies only the keepers to a new folder — your originals stay exactly as they are.

👤

Faces

Add reference photos of the bride, groom and VIPs, or run Suggest People to have Aaria find the most-photographed faces across the day and rank who matters.

📖

Album

Story-aware selection under a photo budget: every event represented, the couple prioritised, closed-eye shots avoided, ritual sequences protected.

Why it's different

Curation, not just a quality score.

Most cullers rank photos by sharpness. Aaria Moments understands the day.

🕯️

Ritual protection

Near-identical frames of different people performing the same ceremony are kept — because each relative's moment matters, not just the first one.

🗓️

Event coverage

Automatically detects each function — betrothal, lunch, reception — and guarantees a minimum from every one, so nothing gets under-represented.

👁️

Eyes-open preference

When two frames are otherwise equal, the one where the bride and groom have their eyes open wins.

🔒

Completely offline

No uploads, no servers, no subscription. Your clients' photos never leave the room they were edited in.

🛡️

Originals untouched

Every operation copies keepers to a new folder. The app can never delete or overwrite your source files.

⚡

Runs on your hardware

Parallel, on-device processing means a full wedding is culled and curated in minutes — no per-image cloud fees.
